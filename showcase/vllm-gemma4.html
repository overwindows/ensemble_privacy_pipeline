Gemma 4 26B-A4B: Closing the MoE Weight-Bandwidth Wall
with int4 W4A16 — at Controlled Quality

Chen Wu · Q:\agentic_vllm + vllm-msn-s2 fork · loading…
Abstract. On a single A100 80GB (sm_80) we push offline gemma-4-26B-A4B MoE serving from the previous best 2045.7 output tokens/s to 2403.18 (+17.5%, up to 2429 in band) through a decode-2D tile/stage geometry, before hitting a weight-HBM-bound Marlin GEMM ceiling (~53% of step time). We then convert the MoE to int4 W4A16 (Humming force-requant — halving weight bytes/HBM) as the last lever toward the 2500 TPS target. Because int4 is a precision/quality change, we gate it behind a general GSM8K + BBH quality A/B: acceptance requires the int4 model to stay within noise of the FP8 control (<1–2% absolute per benchmark). This page is the live results post of that campaign.

Quality A/B — Methods

  • Reusable run_quality.py scores any branch/config via reserved _agentic_eval_* config keys (opt-in; no-eval path stays byte-identical).
  • Greedy (temperature=0) deterministic decode; general tasks GSM8K (+ BBH).
  • Acceptance = int4 within noise of FP8 control (<1–2% absolute / 1–2 σ per benchmark).
  • Both arms share the same fork, tokenizer, truncate + scoring path.
