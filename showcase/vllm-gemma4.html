Gemma 4 26B-A4B: Closing the MoE Weight-Bandwidth Wall
— DSpark Draft + int4 W4A16, at Controlled Quality

Chen Wu · Q:\agentic_vllm + vllm-msn-s2 fork · loading…
Abstract. On a single A100 80GB (sm_80) we push offline gemma-4-26B-A4B MoE serving from the previous best 2045.7 output tokens/s to 2403.18 (+17.5%, up to 2429 in band) through a decode-2D tile/stage geometry, before hitting a weight-HBM-bound Marlin GEMM ceiling (~53% of step time). To clear the wall we swap the speculative draft to DSpark b16 (a 5-layer qwen3 draft keyed on target hidden states), which crosses the 2500 target: at the true S2 8192-token decode it reaches 2634.58 output tokens/s (QPS 1.15). We additionally convert the MoE weights to int4 W4A16 (halving weight bytes/HBM) as a further headroom lever; because int4 is a precision/quality change, acceptance is gated behind a GSM8K + BBH quality A/B (within noise of the FP8 control, <1–2% absolute per benchmark). This page is the live results post of that campaign.

2  Introduction

The campaign objective is a single-worker 2500 output tokens/s on Gemma-4-26B-A4B-it MoE on an A100 80GB. Prior work (this series) established a decode-2D tile/stage geometry (S2, D2G TILE=32 / STAGES=2 / WARPS=0) that lifted throughput to ~2403–2429 output TPS, at which point the model is weight-HBM-bound in the FP8-Marlin GEMM (~53% of step time) — a hard wall for the decode geometry alone. Two orthogonal levers remain: (i) reduce effective decode work per accepted token via stronger speculative decoding (swap the Google MTP-assistant draft for DSpark b16), and (ii) halve weight bytes/HBM via int4 W4A16 conversion. Because (ii) changes output distribution, it is guarded by a quality A/B acceptance gate. This report presents both the throughput result and the live quality evidence.

3  Methodology

3.1  Task and setting

  • Offline decode, Greedy (temperature=0), top_p=1.0, deterministic.
  • True S2 setting: output_len=max_tokens=8192, max_model_len=24576, FP8-Marlin, CUDA-graphs, max_num_seqs=128, max_num_batched_tokens=16384, gpu_mem_util=0.95, chunked prefill, prefix caching.
  • 1000-prompt dataset sc1_delta_v2.jsonl, reps=3, from the datastore; results persisted to shares/users/zxy/autobench/results.

3.2  Draft-swap protocol

  • Identical stack/batch/greedy; only the draft changes (assistant_model_path resolution).
  • DSpark b16 k=7, dspark_draft_topk=64; draft staged on the datastore, resolved at runtime.
  • Comparison honors a strict decode-length-matched rule: QPS and output TPS are only comparable at equal output tokens/request, otherwise the shorter decode looks artificially faster.

3.3  Quality A/B (int4 gate)

  • Reusable run_quality.py scores any branch/config via reserved _agentic_eval_* config keys (opt-in; the no-eval path stays byte-identical).
  • General tasks GSM8K + BBH; greedy, matched truncate + scoring path on both arms.
  • Acceptance: int4 candidate within noise of the FP8 control (<1–2% abs / 1–2 σ per benchmark).

5  Results — Quality A/B (int4 gate), live

The int4 W4A16 candidate halves weight bytes/HBM (0.5 vs 1.0 bytes/weight), the remaining hardware lever. Its acceptance requires staying within noise of the FP8 control on GSM8K + BBH. Both arms are running in parallel on the A100. With the 2500 target already crossed by DSpark, this gate now answers the W4A16 headroom/quality question rather than being the sole path to 2500.

6  Discussion

  • Method differences. DSpark b16 (a 5-layer qwen3 draft keyed on target hidden states, k=7, topk=64) materially raises accepted-token throughput over MTP k5 at an equal decode length (+3.8% @768).
  • Evaluation-harness artifact. The apparent "MTP control faster" reading is an artifact of its shorter decode (~1323 vs ~2291 tok/req) — the length-matching rule (Sec 3.2) excludes it as a comparator.
  • Serving/weight findings. Weight-bound Marlin GEMM dominates (~53% of step time) even after the decode-2D geometry; FP8→int4 halves the weight byte footprint and is the natural next lever, pending the quality gate. Datastore/mount specifics (identity-twin copilotexplore_dtalake_id vs SAS 403) are covered in the repo (Section 7).

7  Limitations

  • The clean 8192-length fractional delta vs MTP is not yet measured (control decoded short); a matched control is the immediate next item.
  • Quality evidence currently covers general tasks only (GSM8K + BBH); agentic/tool-based eval is a follow-on.
  • Throughput is offline single-worker; online batching/QPS at production concurrency is out of scope here.
