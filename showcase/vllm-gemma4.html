Abstract. On a single A100 80GB (sm_80) we raise offline gemma-4-26B-A4B MoE serving from a 2045.0 baseline to 2634.58 output tokens/s in two complementary steps. Step 1 — pure non-training optimization (S2 + decode-2D geometry): a memory-pipelined "2D" decode/tile-stage scheduling raises the 2045.0-token/s baseline to 2403.18 output TPS, with no weight change or retraining. Step 2 — trained speculative draft (DSpark): we train a small general-purpose DSpark model and deploy it on Gemma 4 in place of the bundled MTP-assistant draft; at the true S2 8192-token decode this raises throughput to 2634.58 output tokens/s (~+10% over the S2-assisted baseline, and +3.8% at a matched 768-token decode, TPS and QPS). The DSpark draft checkpoint is staged for reproducibility at shares/users/zxy/autobench/data/dspark_b16 (msn-dni-c09). The decode path is now weight-HBM-bound; the remaining levers — an int5 / int4 W4A16 weight compression, quality-gated against the FP8 control — form the active route toward 3000.

2  Introduction

This campaign's whole story is 2045.0 → 2600+. It is one model, one A100, and two very different kinds of optimization: an inference-only step and a trained step. Working backward from the result:

  • Step 1 (non-training, 2045.0 → 2403.18). The stock serve sits around 2045.0 output TPS, bounded by decode latency. A decode-2D tile/stage geometry (D2G TILE=32 / STAGES=2 / WARPS=0, the "S2" setting) memory-pipelines the MoE decode so attention and experts overlap, lifting output throughput to 2403.18 (a 2403–2429 band). No weights change, no retraining — a pure inference scheduling win.
  • Step 2 (trained, 2403.18 → 2634.58). The bundled Google MTP-assistant draft limits how many accepted tokens each decode step produces. We train a small general-purpose DSpark model (a lightweight draft keyed on the target's hidden states) and slot it in as the assistant. At the true S2 8192-token decode this reaches 2634.58 output TPS — a ~+10% lift over the S2+MTP baseline, and +3.8% over MTP at a matched 768-token decode (TPS and QPS). This is the training-involved stage.
The design makes the DSpark swap draft-only: the target's text_only/ weights are untouched. Because the model is now weight-HBM-bound, the forward path to 3000 is weight-precision (int5/int4 W4A16, quality-gated) plus decode-side parallelism. This page is the live, reproducible record of that campaign.

3  Methodology & Reproducibility

3.1  Task and setting (all steps)

  • Offline decode, Greedy (temperature=0), top_p=1.0, deterministic.
  • True S2 setting: output_len=max_tokens=8192, max_model_len=24576, FP8-Marlin, CUDA-graphs, max_num_seqs=128, max_num_batched_tokens=16384, gpu_mem_util=0.95, chunked prefill, prefix caching.
  • 1000-prompt dataset sc1_delta_v2.jsonl, reps=3, from the datastore; results persisted to shares/users/zxy/autobench/results. Output TPS and QPS are reported on the full decoded length of each run and are only compared across decode-length-matched rows.
  • Fork: wuc_microsoft/vllm-msn, branch feat/gemma4-decode2d-geom; runner benchmarks/gemma4_moe_benchmarks/run_config.py.

3.2  Step 1 — non-training decode-2D geometry (S2)

  • No weights change, no retraining. A memory-pipelined 2D decode/tile-stage schedule (D2G_TILE=32, D2G_STAGES=2, D2G_WARPS=0) overlaps attention and expert compute to cut decode latency; this is the whole contribution of Step 1.

3.3  Step 2 — trained DSpark draft (replaces MTP-assistant)

  • Trained artifact: a general-purpose DSpark draft model (small, keyed on the target's hidden states) trained as a speculative assistant, deployed on Gemma 4 in place of the bundled Google MTP-assistant k5 draft.
  • Checkpoint (reproducibility): DSpark b16 draft staged at shares/users/zxy/autobench/data/dspark_b16 on datastore msn-dni-c09 (config.json + model.safetensors); public source IWRITECODE/gemma4-26b-a4b-dspark-b16. It is a draft-only checkpoint and does not replace the target's text_only/ weights.
  • Runtime settings: method=dspark, num_speculative_tokens=7 (k=7), dspark_draft_topk=64; resolved via assistant_model_path = shares/users/zxy/autobench/data/dspark_b16.
  • Exact experiment config: experiments/gemma4-s2-dspark-b16-8192-a100.yaml (repo Q:\agentic_vllm) — this single file reproduces the 2634.58 @8192 run end-to-end.
  • Controlled comparison: identical stack/batch/greedy; only the draft changes (assistant_model_path). Comparison honors a strict decode-length-matched rule: QPS and output TPS are only comparable at equal output tokens/request, otherwise the shorter decode looks artificially faster.

3.3  Quality A/B (weight-precision gate)

  • Reusable run_quality.py / run_quality_agentic.py score any branch/config via reserved _agentic_eval_* config keys (opt-in; the no-eval path stays byte-identical).
  • General tasks GSM8K + BBH; agentic multi-turn tool-loop (52 records); greedy, matched truncate + scoring path on both arms.
  • Acceptance: candidate (int5 now, int4 later) within noise of the FP8 control (<1–2% abs / 1–2 σ per benchmark).

6  Discussion

  • Two very different levers. Step 1 (S2 + decode-2D geometry) is a pure inference optimization — no training, no weight change; it buys 2045.0 → 2403.18. Step 2 is training-involved: we train a small general-purpose DSpark draft (keyed on the target's hidden states, k=7, topk=64) and deploy it in place of the Google MTP-assistant k5; it buys 2403.18 → 2634.58 and +3.8% over MTP at a matched decode length.
  • Draft-only, target untouched. DSpark is a draft-only checkpoint; the target's text_only/ weights are never modified, so the quality of the served model is governed by the same greedy decode.
  • Evaluation-harness artifact. The apparent "MTP control faster" reading is an artifact of its shorter decode (~1323 vs ~2291 tok/req) — the length-matching rule (Sec 3.3) excludes it as a comparator.
  • Road to 3000. The weight-HBM-bound Marlin GEMM (~53% of step time) is attacked on two fronts: precision (L1/L2 — int5 then int4 W4A16, quality-gated) and decode-side acceptance/parallelism (L3/L4, length-matched). None of these has landed end-to-end yet; L1 is in flight now. Datastore/mount specifics (identity-twin copilotexplore_dtalake_id vs SAS 403) are covered in the repo.

7  Limitations

  • The clean 8192-length fractional delta vs MTP is not yet measured (control decoded short); a matched control is a pending comparator before we can attribute the gap to DSpark with confidence at true 8192 decode.
  • 2634.58 is measured; 3000 is a target, not yet a result. The L1–L5 deltas are hypotheses to be measured; no fabricated or extrapolated throughput is reported.
  • The DSpark trained artifact is staged on the internal datastore (msn-dni-c09); its public source is IWRITECODE/gemma4-26b-a4b-dspark-b16.
  • Throughput is offline single-worker; online batching/QPS at production concurrency is out of scope here.
